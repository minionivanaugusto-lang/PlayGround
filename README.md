## 🐜 Colonia Aurora

A calm, top-down ant colony game for phones, tablets, and desktop. Explore a procedural valley, gather leaves, seeds, and pebbles, and watch your nest grow through three colony objectives. No combat, no timers, no fail states.

### Play locally

```bash
./start.sh
# open http://localhost:3002
```

No build step or dependencies: it is static HTML, CSS, and ES modules.

### Controls

- **Touch:** virtual joystick to walk, tap the ground to move, tap a resource to auto-gather and return, action button to gather or deposit.
- **Desktop:** WASD / arrow keys, `E` or `Space` to act, drag to pan, wheel to zoom, `C` to recenter.
- **Camera:** drag the map, pinch to zoom, or tap the minimap to travel.
- Progress and sound preference are stored locally in your browser. Open the help panel to reset progress.
